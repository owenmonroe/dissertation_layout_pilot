Three Magazines, Sixteen Topics

The Penny Magazine, the Saturday Magazine and Chambers's Edinburgh Journal, 1832–1844

The topics come from a topic model (BERTopic, 16 topics) of 548 articles in 45 issues of three cheap illustrated weeklies. Each text box of an article is assigned a topic, and an article's topics are its boxes' topics, weighted by their words.

Texts are machine-read (OCR) from page scans and are shown uncorrected. This is a pilot on a small sample: the topics describe these 45 issues, not the magazines as a whole.
