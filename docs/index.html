Knowledge Magazines: Articles and Topics

The Penny Magazine, The Saturday Magazine and Chambers's Edinburgh Journal, 1832–1844

This website features articles extracted from a sample of issues from three knowledge magazines. The topic model, BERTopic, grouped article texts into 16 different topics. The website displays the three most reflective articles for each topic and presents each with the title of the article that appears next to it within a magazine issue and the other topics present in that issue. The topic modeling was conducted from 548 articles taken from five groups of three-issue runs from each magazine, randomly chosen from 1832 to 1844, comprising 45 total issues. Texts were downloaded from the HathiTrust Digital Library. The AI tools DocLayout-YOLO and Google Document AI along with code scripts extracted boxed paragraphs of text from HathiTrust magazine PDF files and combined these boxes into magazine articles. BERTopic assigns each text box a topic, and an article's overall topic is its boxes' topics, weighted by their words. Texts are machine-read (OCR) from page scans and are shown uncorrected.

This is an early pilot study of Owen Monroe's doctoral dissertation at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, School of Information Sciences. This was conducted on a small sample of issues, not the magazines as a whole. This study is meant to show how texts and ideas are incorporated and juxtaposed within the knowledge magazines, revealing publisher perspectives, editorial practices, and the genre's structure.
